Nom Nom Sharky - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · App: Nom Nom Sharky (com.nomnomsharky.game)

Nom Nom Sharky ("the game") is made by AngryToasterGames ("we"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game, why, and what choices you have. The game is made for everyone, including children, and it is treated as a children's game for every player.

In short:

  • We (the developer) do not collect, receive or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no in-app purchases and no analytics of our own.
  • Your saves and settings stay on your device.
  • The game can show optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, but only when you tap a "Watch ad" button. Every ad request is marked as directed to children, so ads are never personalised and the advertising ID is not used.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (three save slots) and settings (such as sound volume and control style) are saved only on your own device, in the game's private storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase a save at any time in the game (Settings > Reset This Save, or Delete on the save chooser), and uninstalling the game removes all of it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: for example "Watch an ad: x2 Meat". An ad only plays after you tap such a button, the reward is shown before you choose, and you can close the ad. There are no banner ads and no ads that play by themselves. You can hide all ad offers in Settings > Ad offers.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Because the game is also played by children, every ad request is sent with these settings, for players of every age:

  • "Child-directed treatment" (as required by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and "under the age of consent" (for the EU/UK GDPR);
  • non-personalised ads only: no interest-based advertising and no remarketing;
  • ad content limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating;
  • the Android advertising ID is not available to the game (the permission for it is removed from the app).

To deliver an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the game connects to Google and automatically receives some technical information, such as:

  • your device's IP address (which can show your approximate location);
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, language, app version) and other device identifiers such as the app set ID;
  • ad interactions (for example whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped);
  • diagnostic information (for example crash and performance logs of the ads SDK).

This information is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and is used for showing ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention and security. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Google handles it under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google AdMob is one of the ad networks that Google Play lists as self-certified for its Families policy.

If the device is offline, no ads are loaded and nothing is sent; the rest of the game works fully offline.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration - rumble effects during dives (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Internet / network state - only used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load ads.

4. Children

The game is designed to be suitable for players of all ages and follows the Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (or from anyone). Ads are set up as described in section 2 for every player. Parents or guardians who have any questions can contact us using the address below.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off ad offers: Settings > Ad offers.
  • Play without any internet connection: no ads will load and no data will be sent.
  • Delete your game data: reset or delete a save in the game, or uninstall the game.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete; information processed by Google's ad services is handled under Google's policies linked above. You can still contact us with any request and we will help where we can.

6. Data retention and security

We keep no personal information. Save data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. Information received by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
